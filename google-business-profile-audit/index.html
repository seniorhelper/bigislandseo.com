    How the score works

    What the audit measures and why.

    Why are the items weighted?

    Because they are not equal. The primary category and a consistent name-address-phone move rankings more than a weekly post ever will; the weights reflect what we see change map-pack positions in Hawaii audits.

    Identity and location items carry the most weight because they decide eligibility: a profile with the wrong primary category or an ineligible address does not rank no matter how many photos it has. Reviews and consistency come next because they decide prominence among eligible businesses. Content items are real but smaller levers.

    What does a full audit add to this checklist?

    A geo-grid showing where you rank from each town, a comparison against the three competitors who currently hold the pack, a citation scan for spelling and address conflicts, and a review of the website page the profile links to.

    The checklist tells you what is missing on the profile itself. The full audit tells you how far behind or ahead you are, which is what decides whether fixing the profile alone will be enough or whether site content and reviews need to follow. Our Hawaii local SEO page explains the full build.

    Which Hawaii-specific items catch people most often?

    Ineligible addresses, duplicate profiles for towns without staff, inconsistent Hawaiian spelling, and state holiday hours left unset.

    Rural Hawaii runs on P.O. boxes, which Google will not accept as a business address, so service-area setup is the fix. Businesses that "cover the island" often create a profile per town and lose all of them to suspension. The ʻokina question splits citations in two. And every Prince Kūhiō Day a wave of profiles shows "open" to customers standing at a locked door.

    After the score

    What to do with a low number.

    Work the list from the top weight down. Most profiles move from the 40s to the 80s in two sessions.

    Fix identity first

    Category, name and spelling. These take an hour and decide whether you are eligible to appear at all.

    Then location

    Address eligibility, service areas, hours and holiday hours. Service-area businesses hide the address and set real areas.

    Then reviews

    Set up a request flow that fires after every job and reply to everything. Velocity beats total.

    Then consistency

    Make the site page, schema and citations agree with the profile to the letter. The ʻokina guide settles the spelling question.

    Then content

    Services with descriptions, 20 real photos, seeded questions, a monthly post.

    Then measure

    Rankings from each town you serve, calls and direction requests from the profile, month over month.
